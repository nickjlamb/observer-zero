# Biographical note (~100 words)

Nick Lamb is an independent researcher working on evaluation and verification for AI systems in
regulated and evidence-sensitive settings. He builds open-source tooling for grounding and
provenance-checking machine-generated claims, and writes on standards for the use of generative
AI in scientific and medical communication. His current research uses closed artificial worlds
with hidden ground truth to measure how autonomous LLM agents gather, interpret and transmit
evidence — separating what a population could know from what it concludes. Observer Zero is the
second study in that programme. He is based in the United Kingdom.
