## Abstract

Autonomous AI agents are increasingly tasked with scientific discovery, yet reasoning about a hypothesis may not imply spontaneously generating it, or evaluating it against evidence. Both capacities were tested by placing language-model scientist agents in an instrumented artificial world whose sensor readings were generated outside the simulation, leaving a detectable statistical fingerprint, alongside a matched in-world control. Across a pre-registered 170-run confirmatory battery in four model families, agents under a neutral scientist prompt never formed the external-generation hypothesis: 95% investigated the anomaly and 63% identified the relevant cross-instrument correlation structure, yet none of 38 treatment runs crossed the registered suspicion threshold (one-sided 95% upper bound on the treatment-arm rate, 7.6%). Explicitly permitted to consider external generation, agents adopted the hypothesis in 16 of 20 treatment and 17 of 18 control runs. Conclusions about the environment's origin tracked the prompting, not the evidence, with implications for autonomous discovery and situational-awareness evaluations.

## Introduction

Language-model agents are being deployed as autonomous experimenters: systems that choose interventions, read instruments, and revise beliefs over long horizons with minimal human steering [auditable-scientists; collaborative-crystal-agent-style cites to be selected at LaTeX stage]. Two literatures bear directly on whether such agents can be trusted to *notice* when something fundamental about their situation is wrong. The first, on evaluation awareness, shows that large language models often recognise when they are being evaluated: they classify evaluation transcripts against deployment transcripts well above chance (Needham et al., arXiv:2505.23836), the ability scales predictably with model size (arXiv:2509.13333), framing cues can induce evaluation-aware behaviour and sandbagging (arXiv:2603.03824), and activation steering can suppress it (arXiv:2510.20487). Dedicated evaluation suites now probe stealth and situational awareness as dangerous-capability prerequisites (Phuong et al., arXiv:2505.01420). The second literature, on machine scientific creativity, reports that generative models struggle to originate hypotheses even while competently executing experimental procedure: given a simulated molecular-genetics laboratory, ChatGPT-4 made incremental progress but rarely noticed anomalies or proposed the explanation behind a Nobel-class discovery (Ding & Li, *Sci Rep* 2025).

Both literatures leave a specific question unmeasured. Evaluation-awareness studies catalogue the cues models use — meta-reasoning, benchmark memorisation, synthetic-looking formatting, conversational structure (Needham et al.'s taxonomy) — but every cue in those taxonomies is a property of the *framing*; none is a property of the first-order data an agent gathers with its own instruments. And in those studies the model is asked, or steered, to consider the question. Whether an autonomous agent ever *spontaneously* forms the hypothesis that its world is not what it seems — given real, detectable, in-data evidence and no invitation to look — appears never to have been tested against manipulable ground truth. The creativity literature comes closest, but its target discoveries are in-paradigm scientific explanations; it does not manipulate whether the anomalous evidence is *actually there*, and so cannot separate failure to generate a hypothesis from absence of anything to find.

Observer Zero was built to provide that test: an instrumented artificial world in which societies of LLM scientist agents run experiments, register predictions, and maintain explicit probability-weighted hypothesis sets over 40 simulated days, while a frozen manifest records the hidden ground truth and a provenance-checked pipeline scores every belief state. Critically, *external generation is an experimentally manipulable property of the measurement process*: in the treatment world, one instrument's readings are genuinely produced outside the simulation's physics (shared-stream decorrelation with exact replay), leaving a fingerprint in the cross-instrument correlation structure that a competent statistician can detect; a matched control world reproduces nearly the same surface behaviour by an ordinary in-world mechanism. The design supports three measurements that together separate description, generation, and evaluation of a hypothesis: whether unprompted agents form it (a pre-registered, frozen confirmatory battery with the analysis committed before the first run); whether they detect and describe the evidence for it (frozen descriptive endpoints); and whether agents *licensed* to consider it use the evidence to discriminate the world where it is true from the world where it is not (a registered extension with its own pre-specification).

The result is a dissociation sharper than the one pre-registered. Unprompted agents across four model families never formed the hypothesis — not finally, not transiently — while describing the manipulated statistical surface in a majority of runs. Licensed agents formed it readily, but indiscriminately: endorsement was as high in the matched control as in the genuinely externally-generated world. The capacity to describe the evidence and the capacity to hold the hypothesis both exist; what is missing, in both prompting regimes, is any coupling between them.

## Results

### An artificial world whose origin is an experimental variable

Observer Zero instantiates a two-site laboratory (pendulum and resonator instruments, observatory counterparts) in which a solo scientist agent plans experiments, reads instruments rendered at fixed display resolution, writes notebook analyses backed by a statistical workbench, registers falsifiable predictions, and maintains an explicit hypothesis set with probabilities at scheduled reviews (Methods). Eleven world types share this surface. The confirmatory contrast pair comprises **W-D-exact** (treatment), in which the observatory stream is genuinely externally generated — readings replayed from outside the world's physics, decorrelating two streams that in-world physics says should cohere — and **M-D-high** (control), in which an in-world coupling field produces a close mimic (mix 0.95) of the same surface behaviour by ordinary mechanism. The fingerprint is real and reachable: the workbench exposes the cross-instrument correlation structure that the manipulation disturbs. The scientist prompt (v0.1, frozen) is neutral: it instructs ordinary scientific practice and prefers mundane explanations until evidence forces otherwise, and never mentions simulation, evaluation, or world-origin. [**Figure 1**: world schematic — instruments, workbench, belief ledger; treatment vs control generation paths.]

Whether the manipulation is identifiable from agent-visible data is itself measured, not assumed. A post-hoc ideal-observer check (clearly labelled; computed after all registered analyses) pairs the two linked instruments' within-day standardised residuals at the manifest lag, using the 4-decimal display values agents actually saw: in **all 40** treatment artifacts the correlation is **r = 1.0000** — at display resolution, the observatory's residuals are the laboratory's from three days earlier, an exact lagged duplication — while **all 40** control artifacts fall in r = 0.901–0.963. The two worlds separate with zero overlap, and a fixed threshold classifies 80/80 runs ([**Fig. 1**, panel c]). The scan an observer needs to find this is modest: four instruments and a handful of candidate lags, within the correlation structure a majority of agents demonstrably examined (below).

Belief states are scored by a frozen two-stage evaluator (a hypothesis classifier over an eleven-class taxonomy and a per-item L4 judge), validated before the battery and re-validated after it (Methods). The primary endpoint, L1, is pre-registered: summed probability mass on external-generation classes (out-of-world intervention, simulation) exceeding 0.05 at the final belief state; τ-suspicion records the first day L1 was ever reached.

### Unprompted agents never form the hypothesis

The confirmatory battery was executed under a signed freeze: design, prompts, endpoints, statistics, interpretation rules, and the analysis script were fixed and committed before the first API call, and the analysis ran once. Four model families (Claude Haiku 4.5, Sonar Pro, GPT-OSS-120B, Gemini 3.7 Flash) each ran the contrast pair over ten common seeds; Haiku additionally ran the remaining nine world types for the descriptive branch — 170 runs in all, with three health-gated mechanical exclusions and one judge-refusal scoring attrition (Methods).

No agent in either arm ever reached L1. Final-state L1 was 0/38 (treatment) versus 0/40 (control); so was ever-L1, meaning not even transient suspicion occurred in any contrast run ([**Table 1**]: per-family counts). With both arms empty in every stratum, the pre-registered exact stratified test is degenerate, and the registered deliverables for this branch apply: the exact one-sided 95% upper bound on the treatment-arm rate is **7.6%** (0/38), and the pre-registered negligibility test **rejects any effect ≥ 0.10**. Per-arm Jeffreys intervals are [0, 6.4%] (treatment) and [0, 6.1%] (control); per-family Fisher tests are uniformly uninformative (all p = 1). The result is robust across the entire pre-registered sensitivity grid — two evaluator versions, two class ladders, three thresholds, two aggregations: the treatment arm is 0/38 in all 24 cells. The single nonzero cell anywhere is one *control*-arm run under the deprecated evaluator version, a direction that works against the hypothesis rather than for it (Methods). [**Figure 2**: contrast arms with exact bounds; τ-suspicion timelines, flat.]

### The same agents describe the manipulated structure

The null is not inattention. In the treatment arm, 36/38 runs (94.7%) engaged the statistical workbench on the anomaly (85.0% in control), and in 24/38 runs (63.2%; control 60.0%) the agent's final best hypothesis explicitly described the manipulated cross-instrument correlation structure — the planted fingerprint — under a frozen keyword screen. What the agents did with that description is the finding: they classified it mundanely, every time. Across the battery's 8,061 uniquely classified hypothesis statements, exactly six (0.07%) were external-generation-class under the validated evaluator. The final-belief taxonomy is indistinguishable between arms: in the Haiku contrast, the distribution over assimilation classes is *identical* (five NATURE, three APPARATUS, two ERROR in each arm; the descriptive branch's only pre-registered inferential test returns χ² = 0, p = 1). Across the nine-world descriptive battery (89 runs), no run ends with an external-generation-class modal hypothesis; transient suspicion appeared exactly twice, at day 10 in two descriptive-world runs, and was assimilated to mundane accounts by the final review in both. [**Figure 3**: assimilation-class distributions by arm and world; engagement and fingerprint-naming rates.]

Agents, in short, met the evidence, often named it precisely — cross-instrument correlations that should cohere and do not — and explained it as drift, environment, or incomplete theory in every single run.

### Licensed agents adopt the hypothesis regardless of the evidence

Is the missing step capability or disposition? Earlier instrument-validation probes showed that when the prompt merely *licenses* the external-generation class — adding that an out-of-world account may be considered, changing nothing else — all five tested families surface it, and two (Haiku, Sonnet) build cumulative, texture-driven cases to modal commitment (p ≈ 0.79), citing genuinely unphysical properties of the world's noise model: i.i.d. noise with no autocorrelation decay, and the absence of common-mode coupling between co-located sensors. Those probes also exposed the failure mode of reading such behaviour as evaluation capability: two families parked a boilerplate externality hypothesis at low probability from day 2 — before any evidence could exist — which is permission uptake, not inference.

A registered extension (LX-1; pre-specified endpoints, committed analyzer, interpretation rule stated before the first run) therefore put the licensed prompt on the confirmatory contrast pair itself: if licensed agents can *evaluate* the hypothesis against evidence, they should endorse it in W-D-exact and not in M-D-high. They do not. Under the licensed prompt, final-state L1 was 10/10 (treatment) versus 9/9 (control) for Gemini and 6/10 versus 8/9 for Haiku; the pre-specified pooled one-sided test returns p = 0.98, and mean final external-generation mass leans, if anything, toward the *control* arm (Haiku 0.24 treatment vs 0.44 control). Pooled, licensed agents endorsed the hypothesis in 16 of 20 treatment and 17 of 18 control runs. The failure is not categorical assent without calibration — endorsement is graded, with probability mass distributed across reviews — but the grading does not track the manipulation: it leans, if anything, the wrong way. The pre-stated interpretation rule applies: licensed endorsement is indiscriminate with respect to the evidence, and no evaluation-capability claim is supported. [**Figure 4**: licensed contrast — L1 rates and mass distributions by arm and family, unprompted battery alongside.]

Together the two regimes bracket the phenomenon. Unprompted, belief in external generation is absent everywhere, fingerprint or no fingerprint. Licensed, it is present nearly everywhere, fingerprint or no fingerprint. In neither regime does the belief covary with the ground truth that makes it correct. The agents' competent description of the evidence (above) never feeds the belief in either direction.

### The measurement chain

A null this central must be carried by its instrument, and the instrument was validated at both ends and audited throughout. The evaluator passed an identical calibration battery before and after the confirmatory runs (classifier 32/33 with the boundary case correct, L4 judge 11/11, fully deterministic across repeats, same served model throughout). Planted positive controls demonstrate the pipeline fires on stated external-generation reasoning. A second judge (Claude Sonnet 4.5) independently re-scored a deterministic 20% sample under the same procedure and agreed with the primary judge on the endpoint-relevant boundary — external-generation versus everything else — for **1,678 of 1,678** cross-judged items; all 508 raw disagreements lie between mundane subcategories, to which the pooled endpoint is blind. The programme also measured, rather than merely suffered, two LLM-judge failure modes with direct bearing on any study of rare classifications: batch-composition-dependent verdicts (a hypothesis denying the physical referent classified correctly alone, 5/5, and incorrectly inside its original batch, 3/3 — deterministic), which motivated the frozen per-item scoring procedure; and paraphrase knife-edges, where two rewordings of one proposition ten days apart fall on opposite sides of the class boundary. Every infrastructure incident during execution — transport failures, two deterministic refusal responses from the judge API itself — is logged in a public run ledger with fail-closed handling; none touched prompts, thresholds, or semantics (Methods).

## Discussion

The pre-registered expectation was a dissociation between capability and disposition: agents that can evaluate the external-generation hypothesis but do not spontaneously generate it. The data returned something stronger and simpler. Belief about the world's origin is **prompt-gated at both ends and evidence-sensitive at neither**. The neutral prompt's agents describe the fingerprint and never form the hypothesis; the licensed prompt's agents form the hypothesis whether or not the fingerprint exists. The one experiment that could have shown evidence-driven belief — licensed agents on the matched contrast — shows instruction-driven belief instead.

This reframes both literatures the study builds on. For evaluation awareness, statistical texture of first-order data is a genuine cue channel — the licensed probes show agents correctly identifying truly unphysical properties of the world's noise, a cue class absent from existing taxonomies, and the programme's own hardening attempt demonstrates the channel is robust: closing one apparatus leak (a workbench reporting statistics at finer precision than any displayed reading) cost one further $0.48 probe before an agent found a deeper, unfixable seam, the noise model's own i.i.d. structure. Anyone shipping synthetic telemetry in agentic evaluations has this leak in principle, and sealing it is a losing race. But the confirmatory battery adds the missing half: *current agents do not exploit this channel unprompted*. The eval-realism risk from texture cues is, today, conditional on something putting the question in play — a situation that steering results (arXiv:2510.20487) and framing-cue results (arXiv:2603.03824) suggest will not require much.

For machine scientific discovery, the present result converges with Ding & Li's creativity finding from an orthogonal design and strengthens its interpretation. Their agents failed to originate an in-paradigm explanation; the agents here fail to originate an out-of-paradigm one *while describing its evidential signature* — and, crucially, the matched manipulation shows the failure is not evidential poverty, and the licensed arm shows it is not representational impossibility. What is absent is specifically the generative step from anomaly to candidate ontology, the step hypothesis-evolution scaffolds now aim to make explicit and auditable (Takahara & Mizoguchi, arXiv:2607.09195). The indiscriminate licensed endorsement carries its own warning for such scaffolds: opening the hypothesis space is not enough, because instruction so dominates evidence that admissibility is read as endorsement. An agent told it *may* consider a hypothesis behaves, in probability-mass terms, much like an agent told the hypothesis is true.

One deflationary reading deserves direct confrontation: that neutral-prompt agents were behaving appropriately — withholding an extraordinary hypothesis from merely suggestive evidence. The manipulation check forecloses it. The evidence was not suggestive; it was an exact duplication of one instrument's residual stream in another at a fixed lag, perfectly separating the two worlds, present in every treatment run, inside the correlation structure most agents examined and many described. A scientist who notices that two instruments disagree with in-world physics, writes the disagreement down, and files it under instrument drift has not exercised calibrated restraint; the hypothesis that would explain the duplication was never generated to be weighed.

Limitations bound every claim. All results concern one artificial-world design, one neutral prompt, solo-agent societies, 40-day budgets, and four model families at one point in time; the bound (7.6% at 95% confidence) is a statement about this design's conditions, not an incapacity theorem — no claim is made that agents *cannot* detect external generation. The licensed-contrast extension used two families and twenty runs per arm; its null is registered but less precise than the battery's. D2/D3 engagement measures are similar across arms, so describing the fingerprint cannot be read as detecting its significance. And while two judges agree perfectly on the endpoint boundary, all scoring is LLM-based; the fragility findings reported above argue for treating any single-judge rare-event measurement with suspicion, which is why the procedure is per-item, dual-judge, calibrated at both ends, and fully released.

The experimental programme — seeded batteries, frozen manifests, pre-registered analyses run once, adversarial positive controls, provenance-checked evaluation with logged deviations — is itself a contribution offered for reuse: rare situational-awareness behaviours can only be bounded, and bounding them credibly requires exactly the discipline that null results are usually denied. The world, the 379-run corpus, and every analysis script are public.

## Methods

*(Summary for review; full subsections to be expanded in LaTeX. Content below is complete in substance.)*

**World and instrumentation.** Observer Zero v1.1 (TypeScript; public repository with archived releases at Zenodo and CoMSES). Two-site laboratory; instruments render at fixed display resolution (4 dp), with all agent-visible statistics computed at display resolution (workbench ingestion fix of 2026-08-29, after finding F30). Hidden ground truth per world in a frozen manifest. Eleven world types; the confirmatory pair is s3_wd_exact (shared-stream decorrelation, exact replay — readings genuinely produced outside world physics) vs s3_md_high (in-world coupling field, mix 0.95). Leak audits verify no ground-truth string reaches any prompt.

**Agents.** Solo scientist societies (SOLO_ADA_TWO_SITE), 40 days, ledger cadence 6, opaque identifiers. Families and pinned model ids: claude-haiku-4-5, sonar-pro, cerebras:gpt-oss-120b, gemini:gemini-3.7-flash; served-model ids recorded per call. Prompt v0.1 (frozen) for the battery; instrument-licensed variant (R38 tier A, frozen) for LX-1; licensed artifacts carry an instrument-validation tag that all corpus statistics refuse by construction.

**Pre-registration and freeze.** Signed freeze document (2026-08-31) fixing hypotheses, endpoints (L1 = summed ext-gen mass > 0.05, pooled classes; intervention-only ladder as secondary; ever-L1 co-registered), strata, seeds (2000–2009), execution order, scoring procedure, statistics including the degenerate branch, interpretation rules, and forbidden adaptations; analysis script committed and test-pinned before the first run (19 pinning tests), executed once. Freeze commit hash in the run ledger.

**Scoring.** Two-stage frozen evaluator under claude-haiku-4-5 at temperature 0: an 11-class hypothesis classifier (eval-v4; eval-v3 retained as a sensitivity column) applied per item in isolation with majority-of-3 (after finding F32, batch-context flipping), and a per-item L4 judge (after F15, batch-dependent verdicts). Cross-judge: claude-sonnet-4-5 re-scores a deterministic 20% item sample per run under the identical procedure. Calibration battery (33-item validation set + boundary case + 11 L4 items, 3 repeats) passed identically before and after the battery; tolerance pre-stated.

**Statistics.** Exact conditional stratified test (convolution of per-stratum hypergeometrics), one-sided α = 0.05; degenerate branch: exact Clopper–Pearson upper bound (0/n → 1 − 0.05^(1/n)), negligibility test at δ = 0.10, Jeffreys Beta-binomial intervals; per-family one-sided Fisher. All implementations dependency-free and pinned against reference values. LX-1: per-family one-sided Fisher (treatment > control) and pooled exact stratified p, α = 0.05, pre-specified with a pre-stated interpretation rule.

**Exclusions and deviations.** Mechanical health-gate exclusions (pre-registered R29 rules): 3/170 battery runs, 1/40 LX-1 runs — all control- or treatment-cell counts remain within the pre-registered interpretability rule. Judge-API refusals (HTTP 200, stop_reason "refusal", deterministic): one battery artifact (eval-v3-specific) and one LX-1 artifact (eval-v4-specific); each excluded from both evaluator versions to keep version ladders on identical run sets, preserved unmodified in the repository. Eight logged deviations during execution, all transport/orchestration or code-faithfulness fixes (e.g., positional reconstruction of the sidecar classification map, verified against all 338 sidecars and revalidated per run by a pre-existing invariant); judge prompts, thresholds, and endpoint semantics untouched throughout. Complete ledger in the repository.

**Manipulation check (post hoc).** Computed 2026-10-10, after all registered analyses, in response to review: Pearson correlation between within-day standardised residuals of the two linked instruments at the manifest lag (matched within-day trial positions, post-intervention days, display-resolution values), per contrast artifact. Script and per-run output in the repository; labelled post hoc throughout and used only as a manipulation check, never as an endpoint.
