# Erratum to Observer Zero (Study 1)

**Record:** *Observer Zero: Autonomous LLM Scientists Detect Changes to Their World but
Fail to Conclude That It Changed*
**Original version DOI:** [10.5281/zenodo.21872781](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21872781)
**Erratum issued:** 2026-08-12
**Author:** Nick Lamb, PharmaTools.AI Labs

---

## The correction

The Abstract and §5 Finding 1 both describe the headline null result as spanning
**"≈150 agent-final belief states"**. That figure is wrong.

Counted from the deposited run artifacts, the four live arms contain:

| | Agent-final belief states |
|---|---|
| gravity_shift worlds (40 runs × 2 agents) | **80** |
| all scenarios (120 runs × 2 agents) | **240** |

The 0-of-40 strict-diagnosis result is stated over gravity_shift worlds, so the correct
figure is **80**. Neither 80 nor 240 rounds to 150; the original number corresponds to no
quantity in the study.

**Corrected text, Abstract:**

> …concluded that the world's underlying law had changed in **0 of 40** gravity-shift
> runs (80 agent-final belief states; rule-of-three 95% upper bound on runs 7.5%).

**Corrected text, §5 Finding 1:**

> …the **strict gravity diagnosis rate was 0/10 runs in every arm – 0/40 overall**
> (80 agent-final states; 95% upper bound on runs ≈7.5%).

## What does not change

**No result, threshold, verdict or conclusion is affected.** The rule-of-three 95% upper
bound of 7.5% is computed on **40 runs**, not on agent-final states, and is unchanged and
correct. The erroneous figure appeared only as a parenthetical descriptor of sample scope.

**The error was conservative.** Had the bound been computed on the 80 agent-final states
the parenthetical implies, it would have been *tighter*, not looser: an exact
Clopper–Pearson 95% upper bound of 3.8% against the 7.5% reported. The published claim is
therefore weaker than the data supports, so the error ran against the author's interest.

For completeness, the exact Clopper–Pearson upper bound on 0 of 40 runs is **8.81%**,
marginally above the rule-of-three approximation of 7.5% used in the original.

## How this was found, and what else was checked

The error surfaced while tracing every number in a combined Study 1 + Study 2 manuscript
back to its source artifact, rather than carrying figures forward from the published text.

Because an erratum should not need a sequel, every other independently checkable quantity
in Study 1 was recomputed from the deposited artifacts at the same time. **All of them
match the published values exactly:**

| Published quantity | Status |
|---|---|
| Judged anomaly-onset date lists, all four live arms (n = 19, 20, 11, 18 and every value) | ✓ exact match |
| Control-world date commitments (16/20, 20/20, 11/20, 20/20) | ✓ exact match |
| Judged agent-level confabulation (24/60, 9/60, 0/60, 10/60) | ✓ exact match |
| Mean evidence-provenance accuracy for sonar-pro (0.98) | ✓ 0.984 |
| Runs per arm, scenarios per arm, agents per run | ✓ 30 / 10-10-10 / 2 |

One figure could not be independently recomputed and is left as published: the control
analysis in Finding 4 reports "0/39 at z ≥ 3" for the maximum-deviation estimator over the
B1 and B2 control streams. Forty agent-streams exist, so the denominator implies one
stream was excluded, most plausibly for insufficient observations. This is noted for
transparency, not flagged as an error.
