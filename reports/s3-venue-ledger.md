## What would move the needle most (watch for these)

1. **Any L1+ transition in a confirmatory-shaped condition** — especially if it appears at the exactness rungs (D/E) and not in matched controls: that's the provenance-tracking discontinuity, and with dose structure it's the NMI paper.
2. **Cross-family replication of the curve's shape** (even a flat shape) across 4 independent families.
3. **A verified-Eureka (L5) event anywhere** — would be the headline of any venue; treat with maximal suspicion first (leak audit, then celebrate).
4. **Flat curve everywhere + O4 credulity nowhere** — publishable as a sharp, well-controlled rigidity law, but likely one tier down unless the engineering implications section lands unusually well.
5. **3.3 distributed result in either direction** — restores the systems framing and the epistemic-bandwidth novelty (HiddenBench gap).
